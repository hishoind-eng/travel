Hill stations

Explore the Hills of Tamil Nadu

Escape the busy city life and discover the refreshing beauty of Tamil Nadu's hill stations. From mist-covered mountains and lush tea estates to peaceful lakes, waterfalls and breathtaking viewpoints, every hill destination offers a unique travel experience.

🌿 Ooty – The Queen of Hill Stations

Experience the charm of the Nilgiris with beautiful tea gardens, misty mountains, scenic viewpoints and the famous Nilgiri Mountain Railway. Ooty is perfect for families, couples and nature lovers.

🍵 What can you enjoy?

  • 🌅 Famous sunrise views
  • 🍃 Vast tea plantations
  • ☁️ Clouds and mist-covered valleys
  • 🏔️ Panoramic Western Ghats scenery
  • 🚂 Nilgiri Mountain Railway toy train
  • ☕ Tea tasting at a factory
  • 📸 Excellent photography spots
  • 🥾 Trekking and nature walks

🌲 Kodaikanal – The Princess of Hill Stations

Set around a star-shaped lake and wrapped in pine and eucalyptus forests, Kodaikanal is known for its cooler climate, dramatic viewpoints and a slower, more laid-back pace than the Nilgiris.

🌸 What can you enjoy?

  • 🚣 Boating on Kodaikanal Lake
  • 🌲 Pine and eucalyptus forest walks
  • 🪨 Pillar Rocks viewpoint
  • 🚶 Coaker's Walk scenic trail
  • 🍓 Homemade chocolate and fruit wines
  • 🌉 Guna Caves and Dolphin's Nose

🏞️ Yercaud – The Jewel of the South

Tucked in the Shevaroy Hills, Yercaud stays quieter and less commercial than the bigger stations, with coffee estates, orange orchards and the calm waters of Emerald Lake at its centre.

☕ What can you enjoy?

  • 🛶 Boating on Emerald Lake
  • 🌰 Coffee and spice plantation walks
  • 💧 Agaya Gangai and Killiyur waterfalls
  • 🌳 Anna Park and rose gardens
  • 👁️ Lady's Seat and Pagoda Point views

🪂 Yelagiri – The Offbeat Adventure Hill

A small, uncrowded hill station near Vellore, Yelagiri is popular for adventure activities and orchard-covered slopes rather than sightseeing — a good pick for a short, quiet getaway.

🧗 What can you enjoy?

  • 🪂 Paragliding over the valley
  • 🚣 Boating at Punganur Lake
  • 🍊 Fruit orchard and rose garden visits
  • 🥾 Swamimalai hill trekking
  • ⛺ Camping and adventure parks

🍃 Coonoor – Tea Capital of the Nilgiris

The second-largest hill station in the Nilgiris, Coonoor is quieter than Ooty but just as scenic, wrapped in rolling tea estates and dramatic cliffside viewpoints.

🌄 What can you enjoy?

  • 🍵 Sim's Park botanical garden
  • 🪨 Lamb's Rock viewpoint
  • 🐬 Dolphin's Nose cliff view
  • 🚂 Nilgiri toy train halt
  • 🌿 Tea factory tours and tasting

🐘 Valparai – Plantations and Wildlife

Reached by a hairpin-bend ghat road through the Anamalai Tiger Reserve, Valparai combines sprawling tea and coffee estates with genuine chances to spot elephants, gaur and hornbills.

🦌 What can you enjoy?

  • 🐘 Wildlife sightings en route
  • 🍃 Tea and coffee estate drives
  • 🏞️ Aliyar and Sholayar dam views
  • 🦜 Birdwatching in the rainforest
  • 🌧️ Waterfalls in the monsoon

🌤️ Kotagiri – The Oldest Nilgiri Hill Station

Quieter and less developed than its neighbours, Kotagiri sits at a higher altitude with clearer views over tea gardens and valleys, and fewer crowds at its viewpoints.

🍂 What can you enjoy?

  • 💧 Catherine Falls
  • 🍃 Terraced tea garden views
  • 👁️ Kodanadu viewpoint
  • 🥾 Quiet trekking trails
  • 🌄 Elk Falls nearby

🌀 Kolli Hills – The Hills of Death, Reborn as a Retreat

Reached by a road with 70 hairpin bends, Kolli Hills remains one of Tamil Nadu's least crowded hill destinations, with betel-leaf farms, waterfalls and forest views.

🛣️ What can you enjoy?

  • 🌀 The famous 70-hairpin-bend drive
  • 💧 Agaya Gangai Falls
  • 🌿 Betel leaf and coffee farms
  • 🗿 Ariyur medicinal plant garden
  • 🏔️ Seekuparai viewpoint

☁️ Meghamalai – The High Wavy Mountains

A remote, sparsely visited tea-growing region on the Tamil Nadu–Kerala border, Meghamalai is prized for its biodiversity and near-total absence of tourist crowds.

🌧️ What can you enjoy?

  • 🍃 Remote tea estate landscapes
  • 💧 Hidden waterfalls
  • 🦋 Rich biodiversity and birdlife
  • 🐘 Elephant corridor sightings
  • 🏕️ Off-the-grid quiet

🔒 Manjolai – The Restricted-Entry Tea Hills

Deep in the Western Ghats near Tirunelveli, Manjolai's tea estates are still largely closed to casual tourists, which is exactly why the forests and viewpoints here remain so pristine.

🍃 What can you enjoy?

  • 🍃 Untouched tea estate scenery
  • 💧 Remote waterfalls
  • 🌲 Dense, undisturbed forests
  • 🌫️ Misty, winding estate roads

🛕 Velliangiri Hills – The Sacred Seven Hills

Regarded as a spiritual site tied to Lord Shiva and home to the Isha Yoga Center at its base, Velliangiri draws both pilgrims and trekkers to its steep seven-hill climb.

🙏 What can you enjoy?

  • 🛕 Seven-hill pilgrimage trek
  • 🧘 Isha Yoga Center visit
  • 🏔️ Panoramic Western Ghats views
  • 🌫️ Misty, forested trail sections

🌄 Kollukkumalai – Home of the World's Highest Organic Tea Estate

Reached by a bumpy jeep ride up a near-vertical ghat road, Kollukkumalai rewards early risers with one of the most famous sunrise views in South India, above a sea of clouds.

🌅 What can you enjoy?

  • 🌅 Iconic above-the-clouds sunrise
  • 🚙 Off-road jeep safari
  • 🍃 World's highest organic tea estate
  • 🏔️ Sweeping mountain panoramas
